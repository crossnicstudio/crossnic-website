02 — The Studio
Imagination deserves a world of its own.

CROSSNIC is an independent creative studio focused on cinematic storytelling, fantasy and visual worlds.


We develop films, series, characters, costumes, visual concepts and creative projects designed to bring imaginary worlds into reality.


Our goal is simple: create worlds that people can enter.

03 — Casting

Your Story Starts Here.

CROSSNIC opens opportunities for actors, cosplayers, artists, costume makers and other creative people who want to become part of future productions.


We believe that everyone deserves a chance to show what they can do. Our casting process is open to people who want to develop their skills, gain experience and bring their ideas to the screen.
